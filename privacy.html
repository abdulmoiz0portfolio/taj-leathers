Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

TAJ LEATHERS respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you place an order, we collect the details you provide:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address (optional)
  • Delivery address and city
  • Order notes you choose to add

We also receive your order details (items, quantities, totals) so we can process and deliver your purchase.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process, confirm, and deliver your orders
  • To contact you about your order (confirmation calls, delivery updates)
  • To respond to your questions sent via our contact form or WhatsApp
  • To improve our products and shopping experience

Local Storage

Our website stores small pieces of data in your browser's local storage to make shopping work:

  • Your shopping cart and wishlist contents
  • Applied promo codes
  • Your order history on this device (for the Track Order page)

This data never leaves your device unless you place an order. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone. We share your delivery details only with our courier partners so your order can reach you.

Your Rights

If you have any questions about the data we hold about you, or would like it corrected or deleted, contact us on WhatsApp and we will take care of it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
