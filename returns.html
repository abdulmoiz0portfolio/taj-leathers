7-Day Easy Exchange

Not happy with your purchase? Exchange it within 7 days of delivery — no questions asked.

Eligibility

  • Items must be unused, unworn, and in their original condition with tags and packaging.
  • Request must be made within 7 days of receiving your order.
  • For hygiene and quality reasons, items showing signs of use cannot be exchanged.

How to Start an Exchange

  • Message us on WhatsApp with your order number and the item you want to exchange.
  • Share a clear photo of the item in its current condition.
  • Our team will confirm and arrange a replacement or courier pickup.

Refunds

Since we operate on Cash on Delivery, refunds are issued as bank transfer or mobile wallet (JazzCash / Easypaisa) within 5–7 working days after we receive and inspect the returned item.

Damaged or Wrong Items

If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, tell us within 48 hours with photos — we will replace it free of charge, including return shipping.
